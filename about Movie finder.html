what is MovieFinder

MovieFinder is a platform where you can find movies that interest you. Note: MovieFinder is not a platform where you can watch movies for free, but rather a place where you can watch movies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does Movie Finder get the thumbnails for the movies?

Movie Finder retrieves the thumbnails from the API

Can you watch movies for free on Movie Finder?

No, you can't. Movie Finder was designed so you can search for movies.

Is the “login fumolar” site real? Will my information be shared?

No, the login form isn't real—it's just a simulation. Your data isn't sent to any backend server. Plus, if you refresh the browser, it disappears.
